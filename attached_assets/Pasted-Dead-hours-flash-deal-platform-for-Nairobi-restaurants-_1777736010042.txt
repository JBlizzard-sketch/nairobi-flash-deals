Dead hours flash deal platform for Nairobi restaurants and spas


You are building a full production-grade last-minute deals platform targeting the dead-hours problem for upscale Nairobi restaurants, spas, and wellness venues. Read the full brief, expand the vision, present ~20 phased plan, wait for "next."
The pain:
Every upscale restaurant in Westlands and Kilimani is empty between 2pm and 5pm on weekdays. Every spa has dead Tuesday morning slots. Every rooftop bar is ghost-town on Monday nights. These venues have already paid for staff, food prep, and overhead — every empty seat is pure loss. At the same time, Nairobi has a large population of remote workers, freelancers, NGO staff, and flexible-schedule professionals who would love a great lunch or a spa session at 3pm on a Tuesday if the price was right. The gap between "venue losing money on empty slots" and "customer who'd fill that slot at a discount" is pure market inefficiency. Nobody has built the bridge cleanly for Nairobi.
What you are building:
A platform where venues push a same-day or next-day flash deal — "40% off lunch today 1–4pm, 6 covers available" — and subscribers get an instant notification and can book and pay immediately. No printing vouchers, no Groupon-style race to the bottom on price. Think HotelsTonight energy — premium feel, genuine scarcity, fast decision. Venues pay nothing upfront. The platform takes a commission on completed bookings. Customers subscribe for free and get daily deal alerts by category (food, spa, fitness, experiences).
Critical nuances:
The platform must feel premium — not a discount dumping ground. Venues must be curated and approved. The deal mechanics must protect venue brand: the discount is framed as "exclusive access" not "desperate clearance." Deal slots must be genuinely limited — the scarcity is real. Customers should be able to book in under 60 seconds from notification to confirmation. Venues need a dead-simple deal-posting interface — ideally a WhatsApp bot where the manager types "40% off lunch today 1-4pm 8 seats" and it auto-formats and pushes the deal live. Post-visit, the platform should collect a quick rating. Repeat venue bookers should get a loyalty status. The most important metric is fill rate — the platform is only valuable if it actually fills seats.
Expand this. Consider: a "standing deal" feature where a venue sets a recurring off-peak deal that activates automatically if bookings are below threshold; a concierge tier for corporates who want to book team lunches at short notice; analytics for venues showing which deal types perform best; a "trending now" feed showing what's filling fast today; geography-aware push notifications; and a white-label version for hotel chains wanting to manage their own restaurant dead hours.
Hosting: Next.js on Vercel with edge functions for real-time deal availability and push notification routing. Backend on Railway. WhatsApp Business API for venue deal-posting bot. Mpesa payments. Docker-ready.
